## Architecture rules

- Data access goes through the browser client with RLS; admin rights are enforced by `has_role` in policies. Why: simple app, security lives in the database.
- `profiles` is private (own row or admin). Public pages read the `profiles_public` view (no email/phone; for visitors without an account the name is masked as « Prénom I. » and `strava_url` is null) or the `leaderboard(_from,_to)` RPC. Why: scouts are minors.
- `activities` is readable by logged-in members only. Public pages read the `activities_public` view (km, D+, date, status, sport tag, group id only — no GPX, Strava link, photo or free note).
- Password recovery: e-mail link to `/nouveau-mot-de-passe` (needs custom SMTP in Supabase to reach scouts) and `admin_set_password` RPC for admins (cannot target another admin).
- Activity status is set by the `set_activity_status` DB trigger (Strava link → approved, screenshot → pending; only admins may change it). Why: users can't self-approve.
- `profiles.is_admin` mirrors `user_roles` (trigger) and cannot be changed by non-admins; policies use `has_role` only.
- « Peloton » ranking (group rides): `group_rides` + `group_ride_members` (members-only); each participant links their OWN activity via `activities.group_ride_id`. The `check_activity_group` trigger drops the link unless the author is a member, same date and sport, one activity per group. Group size = approved linked activities; points computed client-side in `src/lib/peloton.ts`. Why: nobody earns points from someone else's unproven ride.
- Birthdays: optional `profiles.birth_day` + `birth_month` only (never the year — scouts are minors). Not in `profiles_public`; members read their upcoming birthdays through `upcoming_birthdays(_days)` and only day/month dates for the exact Staff troupe through `staff_birthday_dates()` (authenticated RPCs).
- Festive theme: settings in `site_settings` (readable by everyone, writable by admins only via `has_role`): `theme` (default | automne | halloween | noel | valentin | paques | printemps | ete | course24h | hiver | carnaval | nationale | foot | tennis | anniversaire; foot and tennis are manual-only), `theme_mode` (auto = `themeForDate()` of `src/lib/seasonalTheme.ts`, or manuel), `theme_calendar` (annual MM-DD ranges editable by admins; photos are edited on each holiday card), `theme_intensity` (festif | discret = no animation), `background:<theme>` (https URL of a hero photo in the public `backgrounds` bucket, admin-only upload). The anniversary theme recurs from birth dates in Staff troupe profiles. Applied as `<html data-theme>`; colors override the shadcn CSS variables in `src/styles.css`, decorations live in `SeasonalDecor.tsx` (`HeaderTrim` hangs under the header). Dark themes (halloween, noel, course24h, foot) also enable the `dark:` variant. Admin preview is per-tab (sessionStorage); visitors can switch animations off (localStorage); `prefers-reduced-motion` hides particles.
- Automatic cleanup: Edge Function `supabase/functions/menage` (verify_jwt off; auth = `x-cron-secret` checked by `cleanup_check_secret` against `menage_prive.cle`, or an admin JWT) runs nightly via pg_cron (`menage-nuit`). It calls `cleanup_database(_dry)` (empty group rides >30 d, past countdowns, pending activities >60 d), deletes proof photos 30 d after `activities.validated_at` (set by `stamp_validated_at`), and storage files referenced nowhere for >7 d; logs to `cleanup_runs` (admin-read). Storage files can only be deleted through the Storage API (`protect_objects_delete`). Never deletes km, GPX of activities, titles or timeline.
- Storage `proofs` (public bucket, 15 MB max, images + `application/gpx+xml` only): new files are named `<user_id>_<timestamp>_<uuid>.<ext>` or `proposed_<user_id>_…` (random part so URLs cannot be guessed); users manage only their own prefix, admins all. The troop mascot is named Alezouille; naming polls are retired.
- DB changes go in `supabase/migrations/` and are tested locally with `supabase/tests/` before being applied.
- Initial admin is granted by email in the `handle_new_user` trigger; further admins via `set_admin_by_email` RPC.
- Encouragement messages after saving a ride: `src/lib/encouragement.ts` (`buildEncouragement`: by distance, elevation, group size → Peloton points via `pelotonMultiplier`, plus a themed line), shown in the success toast of `DailyDistanceForm`. The countdown banner and the home « Mon profil » button take their colors from CSS variables (`--countdown*`, `--cta-2*`) defined per theme in `src/styles.css`.
- Sports: vélo (no tag), course (`[course]` note prefix) and marche (`[marche]` prefix) — `ActivitySport`, `getActivitySport()` and `SPORT_INFO` in `src/lib/database.ts`; icons/colors in `src/components/SportIcon.tsx` (vélo = theme/green, course = purple, marche = sky). `group_rides.sport` allows the three; `activities_public` exposes only the tag. All km count at 100 % in « Tous ».
- Challenge start: no activity or group ride may be dated before 2026-09-27 (check constraints `activities_date_debut_defi` / `group_rides_date_debut_defi`, admins included); the form uses `CHALLENGE_START` from `src/lib/challengeStart.ts`.
- Admin corrections: `AdminActivitiesManager` (Admin → Sorties) lists all activities; admins edit km/D+/date/sport/status or delete. Trigger `log_activity_correction` writes `activity_corrections` (admin action on someone else's activity: modification, suppression, refus — rejection detected via `pg_trigger_depth()`); no client writes. Scouts read their own rows (RLS) in `CorrectionNotice` on « Mes km » and dismiss via `mark_corrections_seen()`. `lock_validated_activity` (trigger `trg_activity_lock_validated`, runs first) keeps km, D+, date, sport tag and Strava link of an approved activity unchanged for non-admins.
- Installed app (PWA standalone **on a phone-sized screen**, `isAppMode()` = standalone + max-width 767 px; on a computer the installed app shows the normal website and `/app…` redirects via `websitePathFor`) is separate from the website: `APP_BOOT_SCRIPT` (`src/lib/standalone.ts`) marks `<html class="app-standalone">` and sends `/` to `/app` (manifest `start_url` = `/app`). App screens live in `src/routes/app/*` and `src/routes/_authenticated/app/*`, use `AppPageShell` + `src/components/app/AppUi.tsx`, data from `src/lib/appData.ts`; navigation is `AppTabBar` (Accueil, Classement, +, Mes km, Plus). `DailyDistanceForm variant="app"` is the light form. All app CSS is scoped to `.app-standalone` at the end of `src/styles.css` — never change website rendering when working on the app.
- Embeddable widget for the unit website: `public/widget/alezan42-km.js` (vanilla JS, Shadow DOM, public views only with the publishable key, sessionStorage cache 5 min). Usage and options in `docs/widget-unite.md`. Keep it dependency-free and privacy-safe (no private tables).
- Expédition (« Le Grand Périple Scout », `CollectiveRouteMap`): stages live in `expedition_waypoints` (public read, admin write). The map lays the stages out evenly by index (columns `x`/`y` are ignored) and the coloured route advances stage by stage. Distance stages (Tour de France 2026 = 3 333 km, Compostelle, Muraille de Chine, tour du monde 40 075 km…) were added by `20261013210000_expedition_grandes_distances.sql` without touching staff-made stages.
- Home « objectif » box (`ExpeditionGoal`, site `variant="compact"` next to « Mes km cette semaine », same layout as the former Earth box: round picture + ring left, texts right, small arrows top-right, app `variant="mini"` in the home card): one Expédition stage at a time (default = next stage, arrows/swipe for past and future), validated km only. Click → site: opens the « Carte & Expédition » tab (the map itself is unchanged since before PR #85); app: `/app/expedition?etape=<id>`. Stage pictures: `stageArt()` in `src/lib/expedition.ts` (by stage id, then emoji; Fluent Emoji 3D, MIT, in `public/expedition/`; tour du monde = `/earth-blue-marble.webp`).
- « Sorties prévues » (group ride planning): tables `planned_rides` + `planned_ride_responses` (status yes | maybe | no), members-only (anon has no grant — meeting points of minors are never public). Insert as self only; edit/cancel/delete by author or admin; responses only for oneself. Triggers: `planned_ride_guard` (no past date, author locked), `planned_ride_response_guard` (no answer on cancelled/past ride, `max_participants` enforced), `planned_ride_creator_joins` (author auto « yes »). Data/hooks in `src/lib/plannedRides.ts` (also .ics export and Maps link), shared form `PlannedRideForm` (`variant` site | app). Website page `/_authenticated/sorties-prevues` (header nav item), app screen `/_authenticated/app/sorties-prevues` (Plus → Sorties prévues; bottom sheet form). Tests: `supabase/tests/test_sorties_prevues.sql`.
- Jerseys (`src/lib/jerseys.ts`, `computeJerseys`): per category (Garçons, Filles, Staff) yellow = most km, polka dot = most D+, white = most km among `scout_year` 1–2 only (`WHITE_JERSEY_YEARS`, never staff). Shown in `JerseysPanel` (site), the ranking badges of `index.tsx` and `/app/maillots` (app). Validated activities only.
- WhatsApp group of « Sorties prévues »: invite link stored in `member_links` (key `whatsapp_sorties`, only `https://chat.whatsapp.com/…` or `wa.me` URLs), readable by logged-in members only (never in public `site_settings` — an invite link lets anyone join a group of minors), writable by admins. Shown by `WhatsAppGroupCard` (site + app `variant`) on both Sorties prévues pages; admins edit it in place.
